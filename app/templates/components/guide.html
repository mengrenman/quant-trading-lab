How to read this page

  • Views: Trade has the chart, order entry, the order book and the tape; Analyze measures this run (the order-book heatmap, stylized facts, order flow imbalance); Learn has the lessons and this guide. The simulation controls and the top of the book stay in view on all three, and the panels take as many columns as the window has room for. Text, in the header, sets the size of everything on the page.
  • Candles are built from executed trades on the simulation clock, which advances with each model's event times and by 0.5 per manual action. Intervals without trades are drawn flat at the last price, with zero volume.
  • Volume is split by the side that initiated each trade: buys in green, sells in red (blue and orange with color-blind-safe colors).
  • Depth of market shows 10 price levels on each side of the mid. Click a level to copy its price into the order form; with 1-click on, a click places a limit order there instead (a buy on a bid level, a sell on an ask level, for the size in the limit form), and the book holds still while you aim. Your resting orders are marked "you"; drag that badge to another level to move them there (they go to the back of its queue). Ticks, in its header, shows instead every price near the mid at fixed rows, bids on the left and asks on the right: rows do not shift as levels empty and fill, and one-click trading and dragging work at any price, empty ones included (Center brings the mid back to the middle). Under it, market depth draws the lots bid and offered, added up from the mid outward over a wider range: a large resting order is a cliff in its side's curve, and the gap between the curves at the bottom is the spread.
  • Your orders stay until they fill or you cancel them: the models cancel only their own. On the chart, a dashed line marks each price where you have orders resting, and an arrow each bar in which you traded, as maker or taker (below the bar for buying, above it for selling), with the lots and average price. My Active Orders shows each order's place in its queue: the lots ahead of it, or with pro-rata matching its share of the level.
  • Your P&L, in ticks times lots (one lot gaining one tick is 1): the strip at the top and the card under My Active Orders show your position, the profit or loss realized by selling out of it at more or less than its average price, the unrealized part marked to the mid, and your edge against the mid as a resting and as an aggressive trader. The line under the volume chart shows it over time, marked to each bar's close.
  • Strategies (at the bottom of Trade): up to {{ max_strategies }} quantitative strategies of yours at once, each trading with an account of its own while the simulation runs. Making markets: market making (quotes around the mid), Avellaneda-Stoikov (around a reservation price, by volatility and risk aversion) and penny jumping (a tick ahead of the best quotes). Trading on a signal: momentum, mean reversion, breakout, queue imbalance and order flow imbalance, each by crossing the spread or by resting at the best quote. And a random trader, a benchmark that knows nothing. Their orders are marked in the order book with their tags. The leaderboard ranks them, and you, by P&L, with what each holds, the lots it traded, its edge against the mid at each fill, the markout (the same 100 events later) and the difference, adverse selection. It holds still while the pointer is on a row's buttons. Fit it over a training period tries every combination of the values you give for its settings on the same period, a fresh run of the model from now or a recording, best first by P&L or by P&L per unit of risk; start the one you like, and its row keeps what it made in training to compare with what it makes afterwards. Strategies trade in a facts collection too, and Reset starts each one over.
  • Models: the bar under the speed control describes the current one.
  • Order-book heatmap (Analyze) shows the visible depth near the mid over the last 600 units of simulation time, with the trades and the mid-price: large resting orders are bright bands, and Reveal hidden outlines iceberg reserves, which the market cannot see. Hover over it for the numbers. It starts over after a reset or a collection.
  • Recordings: Record (next to Reset Book) keeps the book as it stands and everything that happens next, yours and your strategies' orders included, until you stop it ({{ "{:,}".format(max_recording_events) }} events at most). Play it back from Analyze: the run comes back exactly, at any speed, and the timeline moves through it; Rewind goes back to its start. Trading or starting strategies during a playback tries them on the same market, which then goes its own way; Exit playback carries on live from there, with the model selected. Download keeps a recording as a .npz file (a notebook can read it with numpy), to upload and play back later.
  • Stylized facts (Analyze) measures the current model as notebook 05 does. Collect runs 400,000 events in a few seconds; changing the model, its settings, the engine or the book starts a new sample.
Keyboard: Space start or pause → step one event (Shift ten) F fit the chart 1 2 3 Trade, Analyze, Learn In the order book (Tab to it): ↑ ↓ move over the prices (← → between bids and asks in the tick view), Enter copies the price or, with 1-click on, trades there, M picks up your orders to move them (Enter puts them down, Esc leaves them).
