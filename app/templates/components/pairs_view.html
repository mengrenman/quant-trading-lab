Two books

Each book's traders place orders around its fair value. The fair values share a random walk; each also has a part of its own, which order flow pushes and which decays with the half-life. B's part less β times A's is the gap. Neither fair value goes below {{ "%g"|format(pairs.floor) }} ticks: the common factor stops there, which leaves the gap alone.
