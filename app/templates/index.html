Simulation Notes

Candlesticks aggregate executed trades into Open, High, Low, Close, and Volume (OHLCV) bars on the simulation clock, which advances with each model's event times (and by 0.5 per manual action). Intervals without trades are drawn flat at the last trade price with zero volume; volume bars split buy-initiated (green) and sell-initiated (red) trading. Switch between models (Smith ZI, Mike-Farmer, Parlour) to compare how their order flow shapes prices and volatility.

Engineered for high throughput (>150k events/sec) with $O(1)$ depth tracking.
