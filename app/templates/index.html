How to read this page

  • Candles are built from executed trades on the simulation clock, which advances with each model's event times and by 0.5 per manual action. Intervals without trades are drawn flat at the last price, with zero volume.
  • Volume is split by the side that initiated each trade: buys in green, sells in red.
  • Depth of market shows 10 price levels on each side of the mid. Click a level to copy its price into the order form; your own resting orders are marked.
  • Your orders stay until they fill or you cancel them: the models cancel only their own.
  • Models: the bar under the speed control describes the current one.
  • Stylized facts measures the current model as notebook 05 does. Collect runs 400,000 events in a few seconds; changing the model, its settings, the engine or the book starts a new sample.
Keyboard: Space start or pause → step one event (Shift ten) F fit the chart
